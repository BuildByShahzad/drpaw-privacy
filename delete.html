Dr. Paw: Delete Your Data

Dr. Paw: Pet Hospital, by BuildByShahzad. Last updated: November 25, 2026

Dr. Paw has no accounts and no servers of our own. Here is how to delete each kind of data.

1. Game progress (on your phone)

Your clinic, money, gems, stickers and settings are stored only on your device. To delete them, uninstall Dr. Paw, or open Settings > Apps > Dr. Paw > Storage > Clear data. This deletes them right away and cannot be undone.

2. Usage, crash and advertising data

Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics) and Google AdMob receive an app instance ID, your advertising ID, device information and how the game was used.

  1. Email shahzad74303@gmail.com with the subject "Dr. Paw data deletion".
  2. We will ask Google to delete the analytics data linked to your app instance and confirm by email within 30 days.
  3. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings > Google > Ads on your phone.

What is kept

We keep nothing ourselves. Analytics data that is not deleted on request expires automatically after 14 months, and crash reports after 90 days. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policy.
