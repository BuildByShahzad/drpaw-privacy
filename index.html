Dr. Paw Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Dr. Paw: Pet Hospital ("the game") is made by BuildByShahzad ("we"). This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Gameplay data such as your clinic progress, money, gems, boosters, stickers, characters and settings. It is stored only on your device. We have no servers and do not receive it.
  • Device information and advertising ID, collected by Google AdMob to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This can include approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions and diagnostic data.
  • Usage and crash data, collected by Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics: which parts of the game are played (for example finishing the tutorial or opening a new room), app instance IDs, device model, OS version and crash reports. We use it only in aggregate to fix bugs and improve the game.
  • Purchase information, handled by Google Play Billing when you buy something in the game. We never see your payment details.

How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To show ads through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time from Settings > Privacy in the game.
  • To understand how the game is played and fix crashes (Firebase).
  • To deliver in-app purchases and restore ones you already own.

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data was collected, email us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Buying "No Ads" stops pop-up ads; optional reward videos you choose to watch stay available. Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device. To ask about or delete any data, email us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
